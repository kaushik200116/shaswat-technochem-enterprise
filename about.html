About SHASWAT TECHNOCHEM ENTERPRISE

Advanced Polyurethane, Waterproofing & Sustainable Specialty Chemical Technologies

Company Overview

Shaswat Technochem Enterprise is an emerging specialty chemical company focused on advanced polyurethane systems, industrial waterproofing technologies, protective coating systems, polyester polyols, and customized polyurethane solutions. The company operates with a strong focus on sustainable chemistry, environmentally responsible technologies, low VOC systems, bio-based polyurethane approaches, and application-oriented industrial performance. With a long-term vision aligned toward “Made in India, Made for the World”, the company focuses on developing globally competitive polyurethane and specialty chemical technologies for infrastructure, engineering, construction, and industrial sectors. The organization emphasizes industrial reliability, formulation understanding, technical development, customer-oriented solutions, and sustainable industrial growth through advanced polyurethane chemistry.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized and environmentally responsible specialty chemical company in polyurethane technologies, waterproofing systems, industrial coatings, and sustainable PU solutions through innovation, technical reliability, and green industrial development.

Our Mission

To develop and deliver high-performance polyurethane systems, low VOC coating technologies, bio-based PU solutions, waterproofing systems, and specialty chemical products that provide industrial efficiency, environmental responsibility, long-term durability, and customer-focused technical support.
