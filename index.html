About Us

Shaswat Technochem Enterprise is an emerging specialty chemical company focused on advanced polyurethane systems, protective coating technologies, and polyol-based industrial solutions. The company combines technical formulation expertise, industrial performance understanding, and application-oriented development to deliver reliable and commercially competitive polyurethane technologies for infrastructure, construction, engineering, and industrial sectors.

Our Mission

To develop advanced polyurethane and specialty chemical solutions that combine industrial performance, durability, technical reliability, and sustainable technology.

Technical Expertise

Specialized understanding of polyurethane chemistry, polyester polyols, waterproofing technologies, coating systems, industrial formulation science, and PU application optimization.

Industrial Focus

Focused on industrial manufacturing, infrastructure, construction chemicals, PU foam systems, waterproofing technologies, engineering industries, and industrial coating applications.
