About SHASWAT TECHNOCHEM ENTERPRISE

Shaswat Technochem Enterprise is an emerging specialty chemical company focused on advanced polyurethane systems, industrial waterproofing technologies, protective coating systems, polyester polyols, and customized polyurethane solutions. The company operates with a strong focus on sustainable chemistry, environmentally responsible technologies, low VOC systems, bio-based polyurethane approaches, and application-oriented industrial performance. With a long-term vision aligned toward “Made in India, Made for the World”, the company is committed toward delivering technically reliable, globally competitive, and environmentally conscious polyurethane technologies for infrastructure, engineering, construction, and industrial sectors.
