About Us

Shaswat Technochem Enterprise is an emerging specialty chemical company focused on advanced polyurethane systems, protective coating technologies, and polyol-based industrial solutions.


The company operates through two specialized business divisions:

Business Divisions

Shaswat BioShield

Focused on advanced coating systems, waterproofing technologies, protective finishes, and sustainable polyurethane coating solutions.

ShasPol

Focused on polyol systems, customized polyurethane raw materials, formulated resin systems, and industrial polyurethane intermediates.
