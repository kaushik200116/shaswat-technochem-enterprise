Division Overview

SHASWAT BIOSHIELD is the advanced coating & waterproofing systems division of SHASWAT TECHNOCHEM ENTERPRISE focused on industrial polyurethane coating technologies, protective systems, industrial flooring solutions, waterproofing applications, repair technologies, decorative coatings, and environmentally responsible specialty PU systems. The division emphasizes industrial durability, low VOC technologies, sustainable chemistry approaches, bio-based polyurethane development orientation, and application-focused product engineering.
