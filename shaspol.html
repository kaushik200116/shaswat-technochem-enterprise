Division Overview

SHASPOL is the polyol systems division of SHASWAT TECHNOCHEM ENTERPRISE focused on polyester polyols, customized blended polyol systems, industrial polyurethane intermediates, PU raw material technologies, and application-oriented polyurethane formulation support. The division emphasizes industrial processing reliability, formulation consistency, customized development support, sustainable polyurethane approaches, and technically reliable polyol system development for industrial polyurethane applications.
